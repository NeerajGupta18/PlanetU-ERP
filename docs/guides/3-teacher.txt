PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: Teacher or faculty

Signing in
Institute code, choose Employee, your employee ID and password, tick "I'm not a robot".
Install the app on your phone: Android app from the institute, or "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone.

Your home page
- "Today's lectures" is at the top, with your next lecture first.
- The cards show answers to grade, papers with marks to enter, and your students below 75%.

Mark attendance (under a minute)
College or university, every lecture:
1. Tap "Mark attendance" on your next lecture.
2. Tap only the few who are absent (A) or late (L). Everyone else counts as present.
3. "Review and submit", check the names, then "Submit attendance".
   Absent students and their parents are told at once. The button then takes you to your next lecture.
4. Or start a QR check-in: choose how long it stays open, show the QR on the projector. Students scan it in the app.
School, once a day:
The class teacher opens the class register in the first period, taps the absent ones and submits.
No lectures on Sunday or holidays, so there is nothing to mark on those days.

Enter exam marks
1. Open Exam marks. Papers waiting for you are at the top.
2. Type each mark and press Enter to go to the next student. Tick Absent where needed.
3. It saves as you go. Choose "Submit marks" when the paper is complete.

Assignments
Coursework, "New assignment": class, subject, due date and marks, then "Save and publish".
Open it later to grade each answer or send one back to redo.

Class teacher: Attendance watch
If you are the class teacher of a class, the menu has "Attendance watch".
1. It lists your students absent on each of their last three days, or below or close to 75% this term.
2. Choose the language and "Draft a note". The app fills in each child's name and attendance.
3. Open Approvals, read the first note exactly as it will go out, then "Send N notes". Parents see it in the app,
   and a ready WhatsApp message is prepared for each one. Nothing is sent before you approve.

Leave and payslips
- My leave: your balance is at the top. "Apply for leave", pick type and dates, give a reason.
- My payslips: choose the month once the office finalises payroll.
