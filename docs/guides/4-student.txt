PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: Student

Signing in
Institute code, choose Student, your student ID (PRN) and password, tick "I'm not a robot".
The office gives you a one-time password when you are enrolled. The app asks you to change it the first time you sign in.

Your home page
- Cards for your attendance, next class, work due this week, fees due, next exam and latest result.
- The attendance card says how many classes you can still miss, or how many you must attend to reach 75%.
- "To do" lists what is next, most urgent first.

Scan for attendance (college and university)
When the teacher shows a QR code, tap Scan in the bottom bar and point the camera at it.
You will see "Attendance marked successfully". The code changes every few seconds, so a photo of it will not work.

Assignments
Coursework shows work still "To do". Choose "Open and hand in", write or attach, and submit.

Fees
Fees shows each charge, its due date and what is paid. Pay online if it is switched on, otherwise at the fee counter.
Every payment has a receipt to download.

Results
Results shows each published exam with marks, grade and GPA. Download the report card from there.

Certificates
Certificates, Request next to the one you need, say what it is for. Download it once the office issues it.
