PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: Parent

Getting in
1. The institute sends you an invite by email when your child is enrolled. Open the link and set a password.
2. Sign in with the institute code, choose Parent, your login ID and password.
3. The first time, the app asks for your consent. The first box is needed to see your child's records.
   The second, email reminders, is your choice. Your answer is saved with the date and time.

Fingerprint sign-in (Android app only)
1. In the Android app, after you sign in with your password, the app asks "Use fingerprint next time?". Choose Use fingerprint, or Not now.
2. Next time, tap your name and touch the sensor. "Use password instead" and "Switch account" are always there. Up to five accounts can be saved on one phone.
3. Your password is not saved on the phone. After 30 days, or when your password changes, the app asks for the password again.
4. Menu, then "Fingerprint and phones" lists your phones; Remove one you no longer use. "Sign out and forget this phone" turns it off on that phone.

My child today (your home page)
- Each class today, with Present, Absent or Late once the teacher submits the register.
- Fees due, attendance, the latest result and new notices.
- The orange button at the top is the one thing to do next, for example "Pay" or "Read the new notice".
- More than one child? Tap their name at the top of the page to switch.

Pay a fee (three screens)
1. Home: tap the orange button, or Fees in the bottom bar.
2. Fees: tap Pay, check the amount, pay by UPI, card or netbanking.
3. The receipt shows straight away. Download it any time from "Payments and receipts".
If online payment is not switched on for your institute, the page says so: pay at the fee counter.

Results and notices
- Results: every published exam and the report card to download.
- Notices: under More. Important notices are shown in red.

Need help?
Menu, "Help and feedback" sends a question to the institute office.
