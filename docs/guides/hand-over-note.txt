PlanetU Campus ERP
Hand-over note

What it is
One web app for schools, colleges and universities. Each institute has its own data, logo, colours and words.
It runs in any browser, as an Android app, and can be added to the home screen on an iPhone.
Roles: PlanetU team (vendor), institute admin, office staff, teacher, student, parent.

Where things are
- Code: github.com/NeerajGupta18/planetu-erp. The main branch is what is live.
- Live site: https://planetu-erp.onrender.com. It updates by itself a few minutes after a change reaches main.
- Setup and running locally: README.md. Deploying: RENDER.md (free hosting) and DEPLOY.md (own server).
- User guides, this note and the demo script: docs/guides. Each has a PDF copy in docs/guides/pdf.
- Inside the app, every role has "How to use" in its menu with short steps.

What is ready
- Institute setup in seven steps, with templates for students and staff and a check before import.
- Vendor view of each client's setup progress and 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins.
- Admissions board from enquiry to enrolled. Enrolling creates the student, login, ID card and parent invite.
- Fee cycle: plans, dues, late fees, reminders with a ready WhatsApp message, collection, receipt with the logo, day-end summary.
- Attendance: a few taps per lecture, or a QR check-in in colleges and universities.
- Exams: marks per paper, checks before publishing, report cards with the logo.
- Parent home ("My child today") with fees, attendance, results and notices, and recorded consent.
- Public landing page with a demo request form, and a privacy page.
- AI assistants that prepare work for a person to approve: Ask PlanetU with a morning brief, Fee follow-up, Setup fixer,
  Attendance watch and Admissions helper, with an approval inbox and a log of every run. A help chat on the public page.
- Every process shows a step bar with one clear next button. The main pages of every role were checked at phone and laptop width.

Checks
- Automated tests: about 800, all passing. They never call a real AI model. Run them with: node --test --test-concurrency=1
- Backup and restore: scripts/backup.sh and scripts/restore.sh. A backup was taken and restored on a local copy.

Still to do together
- Paid hosting, so the site does not sleep and the database is kept.
- Own web address and company email, so emails reach a real inbox.
- Regular backups of the live database (the free database has none).
- The privacy page is marked "to be reviewed by the institute's adviser". It needs that review before real use.
- Pilot data for the first real institute.
- Online fee payment needs the institute's own payment gateway keys.
- The assistants need model keys on the server: AGENT_LLM_PROVIDER, AGENT_LLM_MODEL, AGENT_LLM_API_KEY for the office
  assistants, and PUBLIC_LLM_PROVIDER, PUBLIC_LLM_MODEL, PUBLIC_LLM_API_KEY for the help chat. Without them they say "Not connected".
- The free model plans may use what is sent to improve their products. Use demo data only until a paid or no-training plan is in place.

Not included yet
Board-format report cards, NAAC, NBA and NIRF reports, official WhatsApp or SMS sending, multi-campus,
offline mode and a custom report builder.

Good to know
- Demo logins are in README.md. They are public and must never be used on a real institute.
- On free hosting, open the site a minute before a demo, because it sleeps when idle.
- No classes are shown on Sundays and holidays, so hold attendance demos on a weekday.
