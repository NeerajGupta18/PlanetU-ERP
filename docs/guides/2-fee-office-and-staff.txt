PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: Fee office and other office staff

Who this is for
Accountant, principal, librarian, gate security and head of department. You sign in as Employee.
You see only the pages of your role. The admin gives roles under Staff, then Staff roles.

Signing in
Institute code, choose Employee, your employee ID and password, tick "I'm not a robot".

Get the app
Open the "Get the app" page (/install on the site). Your device is marked.
Android: download the app and open the file. iPhone or iPad: tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, tap Add. Computer: use the install icon in the address bar, or File then Add to Dock in Safari on a Mac.

Accountant: a normal day
1. Open Fees & Payments. The step bar says what is next.
2. Collect: type the student's name or ID, choose "Record payment", check the amount, pick cash, UPI, card or cheque, save.
   The money goes to the oldest dues first. The receipt is ready to print or download.
3. Online payments tab: check any payment marked "needs checking" against the bank statement and settle it.
4. Remind tab: send the ready messages by WhatsApp or "Send in app".
5. Day-end tab, before you leave: count the cash against the cash total, then Print or Download PDF.

Accountant: Fee follow-up assistant
1. Open Assistants, Fee follow-up. It lists overdue fees by guardian.
2. Choose the language and "Draft the reminder". It writes one message with blanks; the app fills in names and amounts.
3. Open Approvals, check the first reminder exactly as it will go out, then "Send N reminders".
   Anyone who paid in between is skipped. Later the same page shows how many have paid since.

Principal
- Your home page shows the institute overview and what needs a decision.
- Approvals has leave, certificates and applications waiting. Approve or reject with a remark.
- Assistants: Ask PlanetU answers questions in plain words with the figures it used, and the morning brief shows today on one screen.
- Attendance watch lists students absent three days in a row or near 75%, and drafts a note for their parents to approve.

Admission officer
- Admissions board as in the admin guide.
- Assistants, Admissions helper: choose an application, paste the applicant's message if any, and "Draft the reply".
  It checks documents and duplicates and suggests a next step. The reply goes out only after you approve it in Approvals.
  It never accepts, rejects or waitlists anyone.
- Assistants, Setup fixer: clean a messy student sheet. It imports only after approval.

Head of department
- Department leave: approve or reject requests from your department.
- Department attendance: each class and lecture, and students below 75%.

Librarian
Library, "Issue a book": search the book, then the borrower, save. To take it back, "On loan" tab, Return.

Gate security
Visitors, "Add visitor", fill in and Check in. The pass prints. "Check out" when they leave.

Help
Each page has "How to use" in the menu with short steps. Use Help and feedback to ask the office.
