PlanetU Campus ERP
Demo script: admission to result to parent (about 20 minutes)

Before the demo
- Use the college demo: institute code demo-college. It has 120 students in three divisions of BCA.
- Hold the demo on a weekday. On Sundays and holidays there are no lectures to mark.
- Open the live site once, a few minutes early. The free hosting sleeps when idle and takes about a minute to wake.
- Keep two windows: a laptop browser for the office, and a phone (or the browser at phone size) for teacher and parent.
- Demo passwords: Admin@123, Employee@123, Student@123, Parent@123.
- Vendor login: on the live site use your own owner login. SA001 / SuperAdmin@123 exists only on a local copy.
- For the assistants, the model keys must be added on the server first (see the hand-over note). Demo data only.

1. The vendor view (2 minutes)
   Sign in as Super Admin with the owner login.
   - Institutes: each client with its type, plan and a Setup bar showing how far it is in seven steps.
   - Point out the 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins. "We watch every new client until it is running."
   - "Add institute": show the type choice (school, college, university) and close without saving.

2. Setting up an institute (3 minutes)
   Sign in as Admin: demo-college, ADM001.
   - Dashboard: the cards, and "Needs your attention".
   - Office, "Set up your institute": the seven steps with a progress bar. Open "Review the setup".
   - Show the student import Template and say: "Check before import explains every bad row before anything is saved."

3. Admission to enrolled (4 minutes)
   Still as Admin. Students, Admissions.
   - The board has one column per step. Choose "Add enquiry": a name, a phone number, BCA. Save.
   - On the card choose "Fill in at desk" and complete the form.
   - Open it, "Check documents", then Accept.
   - "Take fee and enrol": pick the fee plan and the enrolment fee, choose cash, confirm.
   - Point out what it made: the student and their login, the ID card, and a parent invite.

4. Fee and receipt with the logo (3 minutes)
   Finance, Fees & Payments.
   - The Fee cycle step bar says what is next.
   - Collect: type a name (for example Aarav), "Record payment", cash, save. Download the receipt: it carries the college logo.
   - Remind: a ready message for each overdue student, with WhatsApp in one tap.
   - Day-end: today's money by method and by who took it.

5. Attendance in under a minute (3 minutes, on the phone)
   Sign in as Employee: demo-college, EMP001.
   - Home shows today's lectures. Tap "Mark attendance" on the next one.
   - Tap two students as absent. "Review and submit", then "Submit attendance".
   - "Everyone else is present. The two parents are told at once."
   - Optional: start a QR check-in and scan it from the student login (STU2026001) on another phone.

6. Exam result and report card (3 minutes)
   Back as Admin. Academics, Exams & Results.
   - Mid-Semester Examination, Division A: the step bar and a progress bar for each paper.
     "Before you publish" shows what is missing. Publish is locked until every mark is in.
   - End-Semester Examination, Division A: already published. Open the Results tab, then "Print report cards". The cards carry the logo.

   The dashboard: back on the admin home, show the Year Journey, then tap Institute Pulse's "How is this worked out?". Tap "Collected this month" and show the same figure on the Fees page.

7. The parent (2 minutes, on the phone)
   Sign in as Parent: demo-college, PAR001.
   - The first time: the consent screen. Tick the first box and continue.
   - "Aarav today": today's classes with Present or Absent, fees due, attendance, latest result, notices.
   - The orange button is the one next thing. Tap it to reach the fees. If online payment is switched on, pay by UPI and show the receipt.
     If not, the page says "pay at the fee counter".
   - Results: the report card to download. Tap Simran at the top to switch child (Aarav and Simran Sharma are brother and sister; PAR002 is a different family with one child).

8. The assistants (4 minutes)
   Needs the model key on the server. Without it the page says "Not connected": show the morning brief figures and say the rest is ready.
   Sign in as Employee EMP007 (the principal). Open Assistants.
   - Ask PlanetU: tap "Which classes had the lowest attendance this week?". Point at "Figures used": the numbers come from the records.
   - Attendance watch: the flagged students and why. "Draft a note" in Marathi, then show it waiting in Approvals.
   Sign in as Admin. Assistants.
   - Fee follow-up: "Draft the reminder", open Approvals, show the first reminder filled in, then "Send N reminders".
   - Admissions helper: pick an application, paste a message, "Draft the reply". "It checks and drafts. Your office decides."
   - Activity: every run, who asked and what it did.
   Line to say: "They draft. You decide. They never touch payments, marks or admission decisions."

9. The help chat (1 minute)
   On the public page, tap "Questions? AI help", tick "I'm not a robot", tap "Does it work on phones?".
   Then ask "How much does it cost?": it does not quote a price and offers a demo or a message to the team.

Closing line
"Every job is a short process with a step bar, one clear next button, and your logo on everything a parent sees."

After the demo
- Local copy: run npm run db:setup to put the demo data back.
- Live site: the demo institutes are loaded only if missing, so the new enquiry and payment stay.
  To start fresh each time the site wakes, set DEMO_RESET_ON_START to true in Render (see RENDER.md). Decide this together first.
