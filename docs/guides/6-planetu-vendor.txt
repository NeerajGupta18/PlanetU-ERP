PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: PlanetU team (vendor console)

Signing in
Choose Super Admin. No institute code is needed. Use your own owner login (made with npm run create-vendor), never the demo one on a real server.

Onboard a new client
1. Institutes, "Add institute".
2. Pick the type: school, college or university. This presets the modules and the words (Class or Course, Teacher or Faculty).
3. Enter the admin's name and email. They get a link to set their password.
4. Their dashboard shows the seven setup steps. Most institutes finish in under 30 minutes.

Watch setup progress
- Institutes shows a Setup bar for each client: how many of the seven steps are done and what is next.
- It also shows the day count and when the 30, 60 or 90-day check-in is due.
- Help the one furthest behind first.

Billing
Billing has plans, invoices and payments. Demo institutes are never billed.

Demo requests
People who fill in the form on the public page show under Demo requests. Call them back and choose "Mark contacted".

Configure or pause a client
Institutes, Configure: change modules or plan, reset the admin's password. Suspend stops sign-ins without deleting anything.
