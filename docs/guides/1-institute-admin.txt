PlanetU Campus ERP
User guide: Institute admin

Who this is for
The person who runs the institute's office in the app: sets it up, admits students, looks after fees and exams.

Signing in
1. Open the app and choose Sign in.
2. Type your institute code, choose Admin, then your login ID and password.
3. Tick "I'm not a robot" and sign in.
On a phone, the bar at the bottom has your most used pages. "Menu" opens everything else.

Your home page
- The cards at the top are today's key numbers. Green is fine, amber needs doing, red is urgent.
- "Needs your attention" lists what is waiting, most urgent first. Tap a line to deal with it.
- Every process page has a step bar that shows where you are and one button for what to do next.

First day: set up the institute
1. Open Office, then "Set up your institute".
2. Go through the seven steps: profile and logo, academic year, classes and divisions, subjects and teachers, fee heads and plans, import students and staff, invite people.
3. To import, download the Template, fill it in Excel or Google Sheets, save it as CSV and upload it.
4. "Check before import" lists each bad row and says how to fix it. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
5. To try things first, turn on Sample data. It adds one made-up class, which you can remove later with one click.
6. When all steps are done, choose "Build the timetable". Teachers then see their lectures each day.

Admissions: enquiry to enrolled
1. Open Students, then Admissions. The board has one column per step.
2. "Add enquiry" when someone calls or visits.
3. "Send form link" so they apply from their phone, or "Fill in at desk".
4. Open the application and choose "Check documents", then Accept, Waitlist or Reject.
5. For an accepted applicant choose "Take fee and enrol". Pick the fee plan and the enrolment fee.
   This creates the student, their login and ID card, and invites the parent.
The public application link at the top of the page can go on your website or posters.

Fees
- Set up: Fees & Payments, Fee plans tab, "New structure". Pick the class and year, add each charge, then assign it to the class.
- Collect: Collect tab. Type the student's name or ID, choose "Record payment", pick the method and save. A receipt with your logo is made at once.
- Remind: Remind tab. Each overdue student has a ready message. Tap WhatsApp, or "Send in app".
- Late fee: on the Remind tab choose "Add a late fee", check, confirm.
- End of day: Day-end tab. Totals by method and by who took the money, with every receipt. Print or Download PDF.

Attendance
- Teachers mark their own lectures. You can open Academics, Attendance to see any day and correct a register.
- Reports & Exports has the list of students below 75%.

Exams and results
1. Open Exams & Results and choose "New exam". Add the papers and dates.
2. Each paper shows how many marks are entered. Remind any teacher who is still pending.
3. "Before you publish" lists what is missing. Publish is possible only when every student has a mark or Absent in every paper.
4. Choose "Publish results". Students and parents see them at once.
5. Choose "Print report cards". They carry your logo.

Notices
Open Notices, "New notice". Choose who sees it. Tick Important or "Pin to the top" if needed.

AI assistants (Office, Assistants)
The assistants read your institute's records and prepare work. Nothing changes until a person approves it.
- Ask PlanetU: type a question in plain words, for example "Which classes had the lowest attendance this week?".
  The answer shows the figures it used. The morning brief shows today's key figures on one screen.
- Fee follow-up: lists overdue fees by guardian and drafts one reminder in English, Hindi or Marathi.
- Setup fixer: upload a messy student or staff sheet. It tidies columns, names, phone numbers and duplicates and explains each fix.
- Attendance watch: students absent on each of their last three days, or below or close to 75%. It drafts one note for parents.
- Admissions helper: pick an application, paste the applicant's message if there is one, and it checks the documents,
  looks for duplicates, suggests the next step and drafts a reply. Accept, waitlist and reject stay with you.
- Approvals tab: open each proposal, check it, then approve (for example "Send 12 reminders") or reject.
  Reminders and notes go into the app with a ready WhatsApp link. A reply goes by email.
- Activity tab: every run, who asked, what it did and how much of the daily allowance it used.
If the page says "Not connected", the model key has not been added on the server yet. Everything else works as usual.

When something goes wrong
- Every error message says how to fix it. Read it first.
- Forgot password: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.
- Still stuck: contact PlanetU support.

The dashboard
The dashboard is your first screen. From the top down:
- Year Journey: the academic year with today marked, and seven stages (Admissions, Fees, Timetable, Attendance, Exams, Results, Certificates). Each shows its status, one number and a link to the next thing to do.
- Institute Pulse: a score from 0 to 100 made from attendance, fee collection, pending approvals and exam completion. Tap "How is this worked out?" to see each part.
- At a glance, Money, Attendance, Academics and Decisions: tap any card to open the page behind it. The card's number matches that page.
- Departments compared: attendance, fees and results side by side. Open a row to go deeper.
The figures can be up to a minute old. "Updated" shows when they were read, and Refresh reads them again.
